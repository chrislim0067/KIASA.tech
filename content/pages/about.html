Our Story

Built in Singapore,
Serving the World

KIASA was founded in 2020 with a clear mission: bridge the gap between premium digital experiences and business results. Starting from Singapore, we've expanded our reach to 10+ countries across the GCC and beyond.

Every project we take on is treated as a strategic partnership. We don't just build websites — we engineer digital ecosystems that drive revenue, build trust, and create lasting brand equity.

With over 300 projects delivered and a 100% client retention rate, our work speaks for itself. We are selective with the projects we take on because we believe in depth over breadth.
